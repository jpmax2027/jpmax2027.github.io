PiQuest — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · Last updated: 24 November 2026

PiQuest ("the App") is a daily quiz game built for the Pi Next hackathon. This policy explains what information the App uses and how. The short version: PiQuest has no server and no database. We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell your personal data.

1. Information the App uses

  • Pi username. When you open PiQuest inside Pi Browser, the App asks the Pi SDK for your Pi username (the username scope) so it can greet you and label your leaderboard row. Your username is processed on your device only and is never sent to us or to any third party by the App.
  • Game progress. Your points, streaks, badges, hint balance, and daily results are stored in your browser's local storage on your own device. This data never leaves your device. You can erase it at any time by clearing your browser/site data.
  • Guest mode. Outside Pi Browser, the App runs in demo mode with a local guest name. No account information is requested or used.

2. Payments

The optional Hint Pack is a sandbox (testnet) demonstration. Payment callbacks in this demo build are client-side stubs; no real Pi is requested, moved, or received, and no payment information is collected or stored by the App. If a production version ever enables real payments, they will be processed exclusively through the official Pi payment flow, and this policy will be updated first.

3. Cookies, analytics, and tracking

The App uses no cookies, no analytics services, no advertising, and no third-party trackers. The only external resource the App loads is the official Pi SDK (sdk.minepi.com), which is governed by Pi Network's own privacy policy.

4. Data sharing

We do not share any data with anyone, because we do not receive any data. The demo leaderboard shown in the App contains fictional sample names generated on your device.

5. Children

PiQuest is a general-knowledge quiz suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same URL with a new effective date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy: please contact the developer through the PiQuest listing in the Pi Developer Web Console / Pi Browser ecosystem directory.
